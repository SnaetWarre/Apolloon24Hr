# Public displays

Binnenscherm (inside), Competitiescherm (competitions) and Buitenscherm (outside) are full-screen pages for the TVs at the event. They follow the race live: the runner on the track, the next runner, recent laps, rankings, the label competitions, and on the outside screen a large flash for a new fastest lap or a `Burgie gepakt` moment.

## Sub-features

- `display-inside-now` shows the runner on the track and the next one (`Nu op de piste`, `Volgende <nr> <naam>`).
- `display-inside-recent` lists the last 25 runners in a table (region `Laatste 25 lopers`), newest on top with a `Net binnen` badge. The runner on the track has no live timer here: the inside screen shows the laps that are done. The columns are `Binnen om`, `Loper`, `Deze ronde`, `Punten` (what that lap adds to the runner's points: 20 × time coefficient × time-of-day factor, see below), `Snelste`, and `Gem.`. `Snelste` and `Gem.`, like the ranking averages, use only laps from 20 s to 10 minutes: a double press counted with `Toch afklokken` shows in `Deze ronde` and counts as a lap, but is not a lap time. A runner without such a lap shows `—`.
- `display-inside-ranking` is the top 25 on points (region `Ranking op punten`), next to the recent laps; both lists keep 25 rows so their rows line up. It never switches to meeste rondes. `Ranking voor` filters it to one label. A lap earns 20 × a time coefficient × a time-of-day factor. The coefficient is 1, plus 0.125 for each second under 85 s. The factor is 1.3 from 00:00, 1.8 from 04:00 to 12:00, 1.3 from 12:00, and 1 from 16:00 to midnight. A double press under 20 s and a split half get coefficient 1, because they have no measured lap time.
- `display-competitions` (`/display/competitions`) shows competitions the operator picks. With none yet, it reads `Kies welke ploegen tegen elkaar strijden…` with a `Competitie toevoegen` button; after that the button is in the controls box. The dialog `Nieuwe competitie` lists every label by kind; click two or more (they toggle, `aria-pressed`) and press `Toevoegen met N ploegen`. Teams already on the board as one competition, in any order, turn that button into a disabled `Deze competitie staat er al op`. Each competition is a region named after its teams (`1ste jaar vs Anciens vs Dames`) with a bar per team, leader on top, its laps at the end of the bar and how far it trails (`−3`), and a line such as `1ste jaar leidt met 3 rondes`, `HILOK en Mesacosa staan gelijk`, `Gelijke stand`, or `Nog geen rondes`. A team that overtakes another slides to its new place. Adding or removing a competition resizes the others but slides no row. `… verwijderen` (the × at the top right of a competition, visible under a pointer) removes it. The choice lives in the address as `?competities=<labelId>.<labelId>,…` and in this browser, so it survives a move to another laptop; at most 6 competitions. Next to them, region `Ranking op meeste rondes` lists the top 20 by laps (ties go to the faster average).
- `display-outside-bands` shows the current runner (`Nu op de piste`) on an Apolloon blue band and the next one (`Volgende racer`) on a light band below it.
- `display-empty-track` is what both screens show under `Nu op de piste` when nobody runs: `Nog niemand gestart` before the race starts, `Even niemand` once it runs and the queue ran empty (Timing then says `Ronde opgeslagen. Niemand actief; de wachtrij is leeg.`). The Wachtrij strip (`Wisselzone`) uses the same two texts and `Race afgelopen` after the finish.
- `display-finished` takes over both screens once the race is finished in Timing. The outside screen shows `Race afgelopen` in the top band and `Bedankt aan alle lopers` in the bottom band, with no `Nu op de piste` or `Volgende racer`. The inside screen reads `Race afgelopen Bedankt aan alle lopers` where the runner and the next runner were; the rest of the inside screen stays. After `Race hervatten` both screens show runners again.
- `display-outside-flash` shows a new fastest lap large for 8 seconds, set by the `Recordflits` choice in Beheer › Publiek: `Dagrecord` (the default, titled `Nieuw dagrecord`), `Per 2 uur` and `Per uur` (titled `Snelste ronde van deze 2 uur` and `Snelste ronde van dit uur`; a lap only has to beat the laps of that window), or `Uit`. A lap under 20 s (a double press counted with `Toch afklokken`) never flashes, and neither does either half of a lap split in Beheer › Rondes (a guess, `source: 'split'`). The race's first lap runs a longer course: it never flashes and is no record to beat, so the second lap always flashes. The next real lap only has to beat the laps of 20 s or more that are not such a half.
- `display-look` sets the look of one screen. `Licht` and `Donker` on the Binnenscherm and the Competitiescherm write `?thema=licht|donker` into the address (and this browser's storage), so the look survives a move to another laptop. A `?thema=` already in the address wins over the stored choice, which wins over the default (dark inside, light outside).
- `display-outside-burgie` shows `Burgie gepakt` and `ZINGEN` with the runner on the track for 8 seconds after `Burgie gepakt` is pressed in Beheer › Publiek or on Timing. A press less than 5 s from the newest Burgie moment (a double click, or two laptops at once) saves nothing new: the server returns that moment, so Analyse counts it once and the flash does not restart. Beheer › Activiteit lists such a press as `Burgie gepakt opnieuw gedrukt, telt één keer`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Sidebar links `Binnenscherm`, `Competitiescherm` and `Buitenscherm`, or open `/display/inside`, `/display/competitions` and `/display/outside`.
- Beheer › Publiek (`/admin?section=public`, panel `Publieke momenten`): the `Buitenscherm openen`, `Binnenscherm openen` and `Competitiescherm openen` buttons, the `Burgie gepakt` button, and the `Recordflits` choice.
- Timing: the `Burgie gepakt` button next to `Laatste wissel ongedaan maken`.
- On a TV, the laptop's Event URL followed by the display path.

## Driving it with drive.mjs

Preconditions:

- A run with `--scenario=live` (race running, lap history), or a `ready` run that you advance through Timing.
- A large viewport, since these are TV pages: `run.newPage({ viewport: { width: 1920, height: 1080 } })`.

- **Inside.** `await page.goto(run.url('/display/inside'))`. Wait for `page.getByRole('region', { name: 'Nu op de piste' })`. The runner name in it matches `race.activeRunnerId` in `/api/state`.
- **Live update.** Open `/timing` in a second page and press Space (see `timing.md`). Without reloading, `.inside-now__runner` on the display changes to the new runner and `Laatste 25 lopers` gains a row at the top. On `/display/competitions` the figure of each label that runner carries goes up by one.
- **Double press in Laatste 25 lopers.** On `/timing` press Space, wait for `Ronde opgeslagen. Volgende loper gestart.`, press Space again, and click `Toch afklokken` in dialog `Toch afklokken?`. The top `.recent-lap-row` shows the short time under `Deze ronde`, but `Snelste` and `Gem.` are that runner's real laps from `/api/history?scope=full` (or `—`), and `Punten` is 20 × the time-of-day factor (coefficient 1). Analyse's `Snelste` tile and `Snelste rondes` panel skip it too.
- **Outside.** `await page.goto(run.url('/display/outside'))`. The bands `.outside-band--current` and `.outside-band--next` show the active and next runner.
- **Fastest-lap flash.** Make a handoff whose lap is faster than every earlier lap. Time the press from `race.activeStartedAt` in `/api/state` rather than with a fixed wait: a `run.proof` takes about 3 s and makes the lap longer than planned. The `live` laps are all over a minute, so a press 20 to 60 s after the last handoff is a record and gets no short-lap question. `.outside-record-flash` appears with `Nieuw dagrecord`, the lap time and the runner, and is gone about 8 s later. Laps already there when the page loaded never flash. A double press counted with `Toch afklokken` (under 20 s) must not flash either, and a 25 s lap right after it still flashes `Nieuw dagrecord`. In a `ready` run, the first lap after the start must not flash, and a slower second lap still flashes `Nieuw dagrecord`. Splitting the newest lap in Beheer › Rondes must not flash, even when its second half is faster than every other lap (an odd lap time, so the halves differ by 1 ms).
- **Empty track mid-race.** Start a `live` run and move every `waiting` runner to `warming_up` with `run.rpc().runners.setStatus`. On `/timing` press Space and wait for `Ronde opgeslagen. Niemand actief; de wachtrij is leeg.`; `race.activeRunnerId` is now null while `race.raceStartedAt` is set. Both screens and the Wachtrij strip show `Nu op de piste` with `Even niemand`, never `Nog niemand gestart`. In a `ready` run (race not started) all three show `Nog niemand gestart`.
- **Race finished.** Open both screens, then on `/timing` click `Race beëindigen`, `Verder`, and `Race definitief beëindigen`. Without a reload, both screens show `Race afgelopen` and neither shows `Nog niemand gestart` or `Volgende`, while `race.raceFinishedAt` in `/api/state` is set. Then click `Race hervatten met …` and confirm `Race hervatten`: both screens show the runner from `race.activeRunnerId` under `Nu op de piste` and the next runner again.
- **Per uur.** In `getByRole('group', { name: 'Recordflits' })` click `Per uur`. A lap under the hour's fastest (but 20 s or more) flashes `Snelste ronde van dit uur`. Click `Dagrecord` to set it back.
- **Burgie.** On `/admin?section=public` click `Burgie gepakt`. On the outside page `.outside-record-flash` shows `Burgie gepakt`, `ZINGEN`, and the runner on the track. Double click it (`page.mouse.click` twice, 150 ms apart): `/api/history?scope=full` gains one `burgie_gepakt` event, not two.
- **Look survives failover.** In a linked group of three, open `/display/inside` on laptop 0 and click `Licht`. The address gains `?thema=licht` and `document.documentElement.dataset.displayTone` is `light`. Freeze laptop 0 (`process.kill(pid, 'SIGSTOP')`) and wait for the page URL to move to another laptop. The tone must still be `light`.
- **Proof.** Take `run.proof(page, 'display-…')` on the display page itself, before and after the action on Timing.

## Gotchas

- The flash is meant to stay loud (blue and night pulse, a frame, a yellow slab). A flat yellow screen is a regression, not a style choice.
- Every clock time on the screens (`Binnen om` in `Laatste 25 lopers`, for example) is Brussels time, even on a TV or TV stick left on UTC. To check, open the page in a second context with Chrome's time zone set to UTC (CDP `Emulation.setTimezoneOverride` with `timezoneId: 'UTC'`); both pages must show the same times.
- Only laptops and very large TVs matter. Phone-sized layouts are out of scope.
- Display pages hide the sidebar. Use the region names and classes above, not navigation links.
- The TV pages write many texts in capitals with CSS, so `innerText` returns `NIEUW DAGRECORD` and `BURGIE GEPAKT`. Match them case-insensitively.
- After the finish, the inside screen's region keeps its name `Nu op de piste` while it reads `Race afgelopen Bedankt aan alle lopers`.
- The `Licht`, `Donker` and `Ranking voor` controls sit in a small box at the bottom right that only shows while a mouse is over the page or a control has focus; hover the page (`page.mouse.move`) before clicking them. Only the Binnenscherm and the Competitiescherm have them. The Buitenscherm's look changes only through `?thema=` or the stored choice.
- In a linked group a browser display moves to another laptop when its own laptop disappears; see `linked-laptops.md`.
